My Top 5 Movies

  1. Hidden Figures (2016): An inspiring true story about resilience, teamwork, and women excelling in STEM.
  2. The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind (2019): Shows how creativity, science, and determination can transform a community.
  3. Black Panther (2018): Incredible storytelling, culture, and visual world-building.
  4. Inception (2010): Keeps me thinking from start to finish with its clever plot and soundtrack.
  5. Moana (2016): Celebrates respect for nature, ocean conservation, and courage.
